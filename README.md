# Kitchen & Countertop Builder

An online kitchen and countertop builder I made for NY Kitchen & Home Design Center, a showroom in Brooklyn. Live at builder.nykhdc.com.

**The problem.** In New York, whoever sends a clear price first usually gets the job. A kitchen meant drawing it in paid design software, sending it out for pricing and waiting. Customers got a number days later.

**What it does**
- The customer picks a layout, cabinets, countertop and sink and sees a render right away.
- 105 quartz colors, 15 granites and 7 sinks.
- The customer sends the design in one click. Customers never see prices.
- On our side it produces an internal estimate and a draft invoice.
- A door builder (beta) with 10 configurations priced to match the supplier.

**Result.** Quotes take minutes instead of days. On five real orders the estimate matched our invoices to the cent.

**Built with** Claude, hosted on Vercel.

The code is private because it includes supplier price lists.
